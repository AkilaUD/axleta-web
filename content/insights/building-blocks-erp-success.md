If you have been part of an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) rollout, you know it is more than installing software. It is an architectural change in how the business operates. Executives often ask where to start and how the pieces fit together.

## Three building blocks

### 1. Infrastructure

At the center lies infrastructure: hosts, networks, security, and database services. Even strong application features depend on reliable infrastructure. For SMEs this may include private cloud, dedicated servers, and disciplined backup and access practices.

### 2. Core ERP

The ERP core connects finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, and—where relevant—manufacturing and planning. SAP Business One and SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud serve different contexts; selection should follow business assessment, not a one-size template.

### 3. Extensions and integrations

Add-ons, analytics, barcodes, payments, and custom integrations connect the core to how your team actually works. Treat them as part of one ecosystem rather than isolated purchases.

## After go-live

Go-live is a milestone, not the end. Adoption, maintenance, reporting adjustments, and incremental improvements keep the system aligned as the business evolves. Scope and support are agreed per engagement.

When you plan or modernize ERP, start with which layer needs attention first—then align the others deliberately.
